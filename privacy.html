Backup for server Heraclitus

Privacy policy

This is a personal application operated by its Google account owner for private server backups.

Information used

The application uses Google OAuth credentials, the authorized account's email address and storage quota, and the names, sizes, checksums and contents of backup files. Account identity and quota are checked to confirm the backup destination and available capacity.

Purpose and storage

Google Drive access is used to upload, list, download and verify the owner's backup repositories. Restic backup contents are encrypted before upload. OAuth credentials and recovery keys are stored in access-controlled configurations and recovery materials under the owner's control.

Sharing

The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Backup data is stored with the cloud providers selected by the owner. This informational website does not receive backup contents, OAuth tokens or recovery passwords.

Retention and control

The owner controls backup retention and deletion. Disconnecting the application in Google Account settings ends future authorized access but does not automatically delete existing backups. Recovery keys and backup data are managed separately.

Contact

For questions, use the app developer contact shown on Google's authorization screen, or the project contact page. Do not include passwords, tokens, private files or other sensitive information in public messages.

Effective 26 October 2026.
